Install

  1. Open the downloaded Rostrum.dmg and drag Rostrum to your Applications folder.
  2. Open Rostrum from Applications. Apple has checked the app for malware (it is notarized), so it opens like any other app. If macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet, choose Open.
  3. If macOS still blocks it, open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway.
  4. The first time you record, macOS asks to use the microphone. Choose Allow.

Rostrum updates itself. You can turn that off in Settings ▸ About. Coming from StudyBar? See Support.

Is my Mac supported?

Rostrum needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later. To check, choose Apple menu ▸ About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M-something, you are fine. Intel Macs are not supported.

On a phone?

Rostrum is a Mac app, so it cannot be installed on a phone. Email this page to yourself and open it on your Mac.
