Frequently asked questions

Is Rostrum free?

Yes. Recording, transcripts, notes, flashcards, the Study tutor, courses and Canvas due dates are free. Rostrum Pro is an optional one-time payment of $19.99 with no subscription. It adds the most accurate speech models, practice exams, the Reading tracker and library, Insights, citations, file conversion and email snippets. You can try Pro free for a semester (4 months).

Do I need an account?

No. Rostrum has no account. Your notes, recordings and flashcards stay on your Mac.

Do I need an API key for the AI, and what does it cost?

The AI features (notes, quizzes, study guides, the tutor) need an AI engine. You can use your own ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude or DeepSeek key, the free on-device engine on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, or Ollama running on your own Mac. With your own key you pay the AI company directly for what you use. The developer's own use over October came to $0.43 for 410 requests. Yours will differ. A ChatGPT Plus subscription does not include API access. Recording and transcription do not need any key.

Does it work offline?

Recording and transcription run on your Mac. Apple Speech works on the device, and Whisper works once its model has been downloaded. Hosted AI engines, Canvas and lookups need the internet.

Is it legal to record my lecture?

That depends on your school and where you live, and we cannot give legal advice. Many professors allow it, and some schools require permission. Check your syllabus or ask the professor first. If you have a note-taking accommodation, your disability resource office can help.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

No. Rostrum needs an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.

Is there an iPhone or iPad version?

Not yet. Rostrum is a Mac app for now.

Does the tutor just give me the answers?

Ross, the tutor, asks for your try first and then gives one hint or one step at a time. You can still choose Show the solution, which ends with a similar problem to try. Ross answers from your own notes and slides.

Can it make flashcards and study guides from my notes?

Yes. Flashcards are made from a note and reviewed with spaced repetition. Study guides and quizzes are made from your own material. These use your AI engine. Rostrum can also import Anki decks.

Does it connect to Canvas?

Yes. Copy the Calendar Feed link from Canvas and paste it into Rostrum, and your due dates stay up to date. See Support for the steps.

What is sent off my Mac?

Speech recognition stays on your Mac. If you choose a hosted AI engine, Rostrum sends that provider the text a feature needs, using your key. The privacy page lists every network request.

Does iCloud sync work?

Yes, if you turn it on. Rostrum then keeps its data file in your iCloud Drive. Recordings and attached files are not synced.

What is the refund policy?

If Pro does not work for you, email support@rostrumstudy.app within 30 days of buying and we will refund you.
