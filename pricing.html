Pricing

Rostrum is free. Every feature is included, with no subscription and no account.

What you get free

  • Recording and transcription, including the most accurate Whisper models
  • Notes, flashcards, quizzes, study guides and the tutor
  • Timed practice exams and past exams
  • Courses, schedule, grades, weekly goal and Canvas due dates
  • Reading tracker, library and study insights
  • Citations and bibliography, file converter, email snippets, printing and fixing words from slides

Support Rostrum

If Rostrum helps you, you can become a Supporter. Pay what you want, from $3, once. You set the amount at checkout. It gives you a personal Supporter code and a Supporter badge in the app, and it keeps Rostrum going. Supporters may get extras later. Everything in Rostrum stays free either way.

If you already bought Pro, you are a Supporter and your code keeps working.

Become a SupporterTax included. Ask for a refund within 30 days if you change your mind.

What about AI costs?

Rostrum doesn’t charge for AI and doesn’t limit it. You bring your own ChatGPT or Claude key and pay the provider directly, usually a few cents a day of heavy use. Apple’s on-device model and Ollama are free.

Questions about paying, codes or refunds: see the FAQ or email support@rostrumstudy.app.
