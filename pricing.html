Try Pro free for a semester

Enter your email and we’ll send a code that turns Pro on for 4 months. No card needed. When the 4 months are up, the Pro features turn off and everything else keeps working, including AI with your own key. Nothing you made gets deleted, and buying Pro later turns the Pro features back on.

We use your email to send the code and to stop repeat requests. The hashed copy is deleted after 4 months, and we keep the address itself only if you tick the box. Details.

What about AI costs?

Rostrum doesn’t charge for AI and doesn’t limit it. You bring your own ChatGPT or Claude key and pay the provider directly, usually a few cents a day of heavy use. Apple’s on-device model and Ollama are free.
