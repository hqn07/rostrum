# Rostrum

**A Mac app for the classes you can't afford to miss a word of.**

Record a lecture, get a transcript and clear study notes, then quiz yourself with flashcards, quizzes and study guides made from your own material. It also keeps your courses, assignments and schedule. Your data stays on your Mac, and you don't need an account.

- **Download:** [the latest release](https://github.com/hqn07/rostrum/releases/latest) (Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later)
- **Website:** [rostrumstudy.app](https://rostrumstudy.app)
- **Support:** [support@rostrumstudy.app](mailto:support@rostrumstudy.app)
- **Bugs and ideas:** [Issues](https://github.com/hqn07/rostrum/issues/new/choose) and [Discussions](https://github.com/hqn07/rostrum/discussions)

Rostrum was called StudyBar before version 3.0. If StudyBar is still in Applications, quit it and move it to the Trash; your data carries over.
