Rostrum for iPad

Rostrum for iPad is coming soon. It’s planned as a lecture desk: the slides in front of you, Apple Pencil in hand, and the lecture recording underneath.

Coming soon, and free to download. Want to know when it’s out? Email us and we’ll write back once, the day it’s on the App Store.

Write on the slides while it records

Open the lecture’s slides and write right on them with Apple Pencil. Need more room? Pull out a blank page next to the slide. Every note you write is tied to the recording, so tapping your writing plays what the professor was saying at that moment.

What else it’s planned to do

  • Practice on paper: one page per problem, with room to zoom and work it out by hand.
  • Check my work: see where a worked solution went wrong.
  • Everything the iPhone app does: recording, today’s classes, flashcards and notes.

As on iPhone, Rostrum on your Mac makes the transcript and notes from the recording.

Questions

When is the iPad app coming out?

There’s no date yet. It’s being built after the Mac app, and this page will say when it’s ready.

Do I need a Mac to use it?

Yes. Rostrum on your Mac turns recordings into the transcript and notes, and keeps your courses. You can get the Mac app free now.

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

For writing on slides and practice pages, yes. Recording, flashcards and reading notes won’t need one.
