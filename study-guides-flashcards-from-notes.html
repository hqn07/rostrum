Make flashcards and study guides from your own notes

Studying from your own lecture notes beats studying from someone else's. Rostrum turns a note into flashcards, a quiz and a printable study guide.

Flashcards

  • Make cards from any note in a click. These use your AI engine.
  • Review with spaced repetition, so cards you know come back less often.
  • Questions you miss in a quiz come back the next day as flashcards.
  • Import Anki decks (.apkg) or CSV text, and export decks back to Anki.

Study guides

A guide is built from your own notes and the course material you add. It includes:

  • A worked example, then one to finish, then one to do yourself.
  • Cover-and-recall questions.
  • A page of your own mistakes from quizzes.
  • Space to write when you print it.

You choose the length, split it into titled parts and pick what goes in.

Quizzes and practice exams

Quizzes are made from your material and drop broken or giveaway questions. Practice exams with a timer are part of Rostrum Pro.

What to know

Cards, quizzes and guides are written by an AI engine you choose. They are only as good as your notes and the model, so skim them before you trust them. What the AI costs.
