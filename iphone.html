Rostrum for iPhone

A free Rostrum Companion app for iPhone is coming soon. Record a lecture on your phone, and Rostrum on your Mac turns it into a transcript and notes.

Coming soon, and free to download. Want to know when it’s out? Email us and we’ll write back once, the day it’s on the App Store.

Record lectures on your iPhone

Put your phone at the front of the room, closer to the professor than your laptop could be, and press record. Star a moment or snap a photo of the board while it records. Back at your Mac, the recording comes over and Rostrum writes the transcript and notes with the same transcription model it uses for Mac recordings.

What else it’s planned to do

  • Today: your next class and what’s due, from the same courses and Canvas due dates you set up on the Mac.
  • Schedule: your day or week at a glance.
  • Flashcards to review between classes.
  • Your notes to read on the go.

Questions

When is the iPhone app coming out?

There’s no date yet. It’s being built after the Mac app, and this page will say when it’s ready.

Will the iPhone app cost anything?

No. Rostrum Companion for iPhone will be free, with nothing to buy inside it. Supporter is optional on the Mac.

Do I need a Mac to use it?

Yes. Rostrum on your Mac turns recordings into the transcript and notes, and keeps your courses. You can get the Mac app free now.

Why does the Mac make the notes?

A Mac runs the most accurate transcription model, and your notes are only as good as the transcript. The phone app won’t settle for a worse transcript just to do it on the phone.
