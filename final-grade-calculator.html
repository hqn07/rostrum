What do you need on the final to get the grade you want? Enter your categories, what each is worth and your score so far.

CategoryWeight %Your score %

This page runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or saved. It uses the usual weighted average: each category's score times its weight, added up.

How it works

Your grade is the sum of each category's score times its weight, divided by the total weight. If the final is worth 40% and you want 90% overall, the calculator solves for the final score that makes the total come out right. Check your syllabus for rules this page does not know about, such as dropped lowest scores or a different letter scale.
